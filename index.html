03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I'm
Jenna.

Creative production is where organization meets imagination.

After graduating from Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD) with a degree in Commercial Photography, I began my career as an assistant photographer. I soon discovered that production was my true passion.

Since then, I've worked on projects from concept through completion, including national campaigns, PDP, corporate events, and Marcom and e-commerce productions — both in the studio and on location.

My experience spans equipment, scheduling, talent and crew coordination, shot lists, partner communication, and post-production oversight. I love bringing the moving pieces together and finding creative solutions along the way.

I'm always excited to collaborate with people who value creativity, professionalism, and attention to detail.
